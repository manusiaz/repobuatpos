Role system

Every account
gets its own app.

Owners, managers, cashiers, kitchen staff, waiters, BorNiz admins and customers do different jobs on different devices. Each role gets its own shell, navigation, home screen, density and accent. Status colors, type and components stay shared, so the product still feels like one system.

Managers have the owner’s tools; “!” in the Manager column means the owner approves before it goes live (price changes, publishing studio changes, payroll, profit view, refunds over IDR 500k). Tap a cell to cycle Allowed → Own outlet / shift → Needs approval → Hidden. An orange dot means it differs from the preset. Locked cells can’t change: the Owner and BorNiz admin columns, and owner-only actions (prices, publishing, payroll, profit, billing, users & roles, refunds over IDR 500k) where the manager can at most request and staff stay hidden. Saving needs the owner’s approval PIN, is logged, and applies from each person’s next sign-in. Per-person exceptions and custom roles (Head barista, Supervisor) live in Settings → Users & roles and start from this matrix.
