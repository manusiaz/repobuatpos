S2 — Layout grids

Where things sit

Dashboard · 12 col
248
top bar 68
Content max 1440px, margin 24px, gutter 16px. KPI rows use 3 or 4 columns, charts 8 + 4, tables span all 12. Below 1024px the sidebar collapses to a 76px rail.
POS register · split
rail
Product grid on the left: auto-fill 160–200px tiles, 12px gap. Cart on the right: 360–420px fixed. The Pay button stays pinned to the bottom. Left-handed mode mirrors the layout.
Kitchen display · ticket columns
Tickets are 300px wide and flow left to right, oldest first. Long tickets span two rows. The header strip shows station, all-day count and clock. No scrolling: overflow pages after 10s.
Mobile · 4 col
Margin 16px, gutter 12px. Respect the safe area at the top and bottom. Primary actions sit in a bottom bar above the home indicator, 56px tall. Menu cards are full width; category chips scroll horizontally.
