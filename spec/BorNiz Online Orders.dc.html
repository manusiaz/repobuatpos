Ride-hailing & online orders

GoFood, GrabFood, ShopeeFood, the web store and QR orders arrive by themselves. Nobody types them. Every order gets a channel, a service tier and a ready-by time, and the kitchen cooks by ready-by time, not by arrival. Delivery and dine-in run in separate lanes so instant orders don’t push tables back.
