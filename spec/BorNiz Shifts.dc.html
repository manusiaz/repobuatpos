Shift system

Different crew,
same counter.

An outlet runs morning, afternoon and night shifts with different people on each. BorNiz ties every sale, cash count, void and ticket to a shift and a person. Devices stay signed in to the outlet; people come and go with a PIN. This page shows how shifts look on every surface.
